Research interests

Emergency coordination

How organizations share tasks, act on warnings, and adapt coordination across regions as disasters evolve.

Relief logistics

How relief teams can adapt deliveries to road disruptions while accounting for urgent needs and work already under way.

Natural hazard risk

Using spatial data and interpretable machine learning to examine questions such as landslide susceptibility.

Selected publications

Sustainability · 2026 · First author

A Multiscale Review of Emergency Collaboration Across Natural Hazards

Aoxin Yang, Daming Wu, Beinan Guo, Zixiao Li, Shaofei Lan, Peng Lu

Full text (DOI)
Research summary

This review compares emergency collaboration across spatial, organizational, temporal, and task scales. It examines how responders turn warnings into action and when they need to redistribute work as conditions change.

Drones · 2026

Execution-Consistent Event-Responsive Truck–UAV Coordination Under Simulated Progressive Road Disruption

Peng Lu, Daming Wu, Zixiao Li, Beinan Guo, Shaofei Lan, Aoxin Yang

Full text (DOI)
Research summary

This simulation study examines how truck and drone teams can revise relief delivery plans as road blockages become known while preserving service commitments already in progress.

Land · 2026

Landslide Susceptibility Assessment in Badong County, Hubei Province, Based on Machine Learning and SHAP Interpretability Analysis

Shaofei Lan, Daming Wu, Peng Lu, Aoxin Yang, Beinan Guo, Zixiao Li

Full text (DOI)
Research summary

The study uses machine learning to assess landslide susceptibility in Badong County, Hubei, and SHAP to interpret how different factors contribute to model predictions.
